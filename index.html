Archival Statement & Methodology

Polly's Tree represents a rigorous intersection of oral histories preserved within family repositories and public documentary proof. By structuring the database into dual-provenance pathways, the project contrasts memory against the institutional archive, revealing discrepancies, silent omissions, and truths hidden within the folds of historical records.

Every dynamic entity linked herein serves to construct a matrix of accountability and remembrance for generations to come.
