Born September 9, 1905, Hattie’s daughter Ida Belle McDaniel turned literacy into a four-decade career as a public school teacher. On August 18, 1930, she signed a contract with Hanover County to teach at Doswell Colored School ($45/mo) and later completed graduate coursework at Virginia Union University (1946).

Ida gathered and guarded every deed, tax receipt, and court letter inside Aunt Marilyn's brown leather suitcase, passing it down so the family's true history could never be erased.
