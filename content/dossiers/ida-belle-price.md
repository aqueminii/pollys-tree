Born on the family farmstead in Doswell, Hanover County, Ida Belle McDaniel Price grew up listening to her grandmother Hattie Belle recount the stories of Polly Smith's orchard in Earlysville. 

A graduate of the Hanover County training school, she became the family's lead oral historian and document preservationist. In 1934, she married Thomas Price, expanding the home into a sanctuary for extensive family reunions. In her later years, she accurately charted five generations of descent, directly tracing the family's resilience back to Polly Smith's 1865 emancipation.
