Daughter of Polly and David Smith, Elizabeth 'Lizzie' Smith served as the foundational link connecting the family's rural Rivanna homestead to Charlottesville's urban core. 

On December 13, 1878, she married Bigeton Newton, establishing their family residence at 303 North 3rd Street in the historic Vinegar Hill neighborhood. She gave birth to daughters Hattie Belle and Mattie before passing away from typhoid fever in November 1881 at the age of 26.
