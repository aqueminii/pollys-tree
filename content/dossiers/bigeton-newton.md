Husband of Elizabeth 'Lizzie' Smith and father to Hattie Belle Newton, Bigeton acquired real estate at 303 North 3rd Street in Charlottesville's Vinegar Hill during Reconstruction. 

His sudden passing without a will in 1903 triggered the multi-decade Chancery litigation *McDaniel v. Newton*, making his estate the center of the family's defense of property rights.
