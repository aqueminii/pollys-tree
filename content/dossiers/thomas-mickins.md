Working in lockstep with Hattie McDaniel following Bigeton Newton’s death in 1903, Thomas Mickins installed a tenant in 303 N. 3rd Street, collected monthly rents, and paid the undertaker’s funeral bill directly. 

This eliminated a primary debt leverage point: by satisfying the undertaker's claim out of pocket, Mickins and Hattie prevented predatory law firms from using unpaid debts to force an immediate auction of the property or inflate court-administered fee structures.
