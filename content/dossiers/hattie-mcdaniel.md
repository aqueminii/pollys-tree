Raised by her grandmother Polly Smith following her mother Lizzie's early passing, Hattie Belle Newton McDaniel became the literate defender of the family's land and legal rights.

Following Bigeton Newton's passing in 1903, Hattie audited Chancery partition filings, confronted predatory court commissioners in Charlottesville, and preserved crucial correspondence proving title claims. Partnering with Alexander McDaniel Sr., she reinvested family assets into 47.5 acres in Doswell, Hanover County, establishing the permanent generational sanctuary.
