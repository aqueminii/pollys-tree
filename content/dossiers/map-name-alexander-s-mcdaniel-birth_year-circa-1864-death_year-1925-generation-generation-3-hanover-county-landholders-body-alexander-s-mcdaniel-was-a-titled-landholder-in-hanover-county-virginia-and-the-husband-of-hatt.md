Alexander S. McDaniel was a titled landholder in Hanover County, Virginia, and the husband of Hattie Newton McDaniel. 



Together with Hattie, Alexander secured and maintained 25 acres of titled land in Doswell (Hanover County) in the late 1890s and early 1900s, anchoring the family’s agricultural and residential autonomy outside of Richmond through the Jim Crow era.
