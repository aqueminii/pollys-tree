Partner and husband to Hattie Belle Newton, Alexander McDaniel co-founded the 47.5-acre agricultural sanctuary in Doswell, Hanover County. 

Managing timber rights, livestock, and commercial banking relationships, Alexander provided the physical and economic muscle that secured complete independence outside predatory sharecropping systems.
