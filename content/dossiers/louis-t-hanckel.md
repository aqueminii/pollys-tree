Represented plaintiff Hattie B. McDaniel in *McDaniel v. Newton* and served as Special Commissioner executing the auction of 303 N. 3rd St. 

While Hanckel reported in court filings that he held missing heir John Newton's share in escrow, audits show the firm systematically absorbed portions of the equity into ongoing administrative and legal fees.
