Official marriage certificate uniting Hattie Belle Newton and Alexander S. McDaniel on January 19, 1903.

**Document Details:**
* **Groom:** Alexander McDaniel, Richmond / Hanover County
* **Bride:** Hattie Belle Newton, Charlottesville
* **Parents of Bride:** Bigeton Newton and Lizzie Newton
* **Significance:** Documents the union that created the bridge between Charlottesville’s urban core and the rural sanctuary established in Hanover County.
