Original sheriff's summons served on Polly Smith arising from the Chancery litigation *Brown’s Administrator v. Burnley*, administered by the Duke Law Firm.

**Transcript & Context:**
Summons issued by the Albemarle County Sheriff commanding Polly Smith to answer claims regarding deferred purchase installments on the Earlysville orchard parcel. 

**Significance:** Demonstrates the ongoing legal harassment faced by Polly Smith from court-appointed special commissioners, despite private receipts preserved in the family suitcase showing regular cash payments toward the land.
