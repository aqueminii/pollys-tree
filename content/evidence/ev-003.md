Two-page formal notice and correspondence addressed to Polly Smith regarding the legal boundaries and payment status of her Earlysville orchard tract.

**Significance:** Corroborates the family's active financial maintenance of the 1889 Earlysville orchard acreage and documents the involvement of her son Isaac Smith in family business affairs.
